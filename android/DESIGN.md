# BRAND CONTRACT: VitalFi Tactical Search & Rescue

## 1. COLOR SYSTEM (High-Contrast Field Operations)
- Primary Background: `#070D08` (Deep Tactical Night)
- Card/Panel Background: `#111A13` (Muted Tactical Olive/Slate)
- Border / Stroke Tokens: `#1E3322` (Subtle boundary), `#00FFCC` (Active Cyber Cyan)
- Primary Accent (Cyan): `#00FFCC` (CSI phase, bearings, navigation vectors)
- Secondary Accent (Emerald): `#66FF99` (Safe / Clear, offline ready, active Wi-Fi)
- Warning Accent (Safety Amber): `#FFCC00` (Calibration, approaching alerts)
- Distress Accent (Critical Red): `#FF3366` (Immobile breathing, active distress, trap alert)
- Text Contrast: `#FFFFFF` (Primary Data), `#A0BBA0` (Secondary Labels), `#668866` (Sub-hints)

## 2. TYPOGRAPHY & DATA DENSITY
- Metric Headings: Monospace Bold (`14sp - 16sp`) for instantaneous readability in sunlight or smoke.
- Navigation Prompts: Bold, High-contrast (`13sp - 15sp`) with clear directional arrows (`⬆️`, `↗️`, `↖️`).
- Badges & Units: Monospace Medium (`10sp - 11sp`) with explicit physical units (`m`, `rpm`, `dBm`, `°`, `Hz`).

## 3. COMPONENT PATTERNS & FIELD ERGONOMICS
- Minimum Touch Target: `48dp` on all actionable buttons (Glove-compatible).
- Hero Status Banner: Instant color-coded recognition of vital states (`CLEAR`, `BREATHING`, `DISTRESS`, `CALIBRATING`).
- Waveform & Spectrum Visualizers: Seamless embedded Canvas with dynamic amplitude autoscaling and zero jitter.
- Expandable Controls: Secondary tools (Rubble zone exclusion, rescuer filters) are grouped cleanly to avoid screen clutter while maintaining fast 1-tap access.
